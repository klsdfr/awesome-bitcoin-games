There was no good single list of Bitcoin games, so here's one — kept **honest and
curated**. The focus is games where Bitcoin is the **setting, story, or joke** (push
blocks, mine, dodge central bankers, dig a landfill for a lost hard drive). Games that
pay out **real sats** are a different beast — they live in their own clearly-labelled
[Earn real sats](#-earn-real-sats-lightning) section so they never get mixed in.

> Every entry is **verified live** when added. Dead or migrated projects move to
> [Historical](#%EF%B8%8F-historical--no-longer-bitcoin) rather than vanishing — honesty over link-count.

## 🕹️ Play in your browser
Instant, no install, no wallet — just play.

- ⭐ **[Bitcoinia](https://bitcoinia.org)** — Retro arcade-puzzler: push ledger blocks,
  stack sats and out-maze the fiat villains across 50 levels, on a server-verified,
  cheat-proof leaderboard. `🌐 Browser` `🆓 Free` `🏆 Leaderboard` _(maintained by this list's authors)_
- **[Shitcoin Survivors](https://mythography.itch.io/shitcoinsurvivors)** — Vampire-Survivors-style
  roguelite where a Bitcoin maxi cleanses the "Shitcoin Swamps." `🌐 Browser` `📱 Android` `🆓 Free`
- **[CryptoDefender](https://satoshininjamoto.itch.io/cryptodefender)** — Survive waves
  of "Central Bankers" coming for your coins, across three 90-second levels. `🌐 Browser` `🆓 Free`
- **[Bitcoin Catcher](https://candyarcade.itch.io/bitcoin-catcher)** — Casual catcher:
  grab the falling bitcoins, dodge the market crash. `🌐 Browser` `📱 Android` `🆓 Free`
- **[Indie Joe — Find The Bitcoin](https://helixgem.itch.io/indie-joe-find-the-bitcoin)** —
  Short 2D platformer hunting a hidden Bitcoin artifact. `🌐 Browser` `🆓 Free`
- **[Bitcoin Clicker](https://nightcubegames.itch.io/bitcoin-clicker)** — Minimal idle
  clicker: tap the coin, buy upgrades, repeat. `🌐 Browser` `🆓 Free`

## 🎮 On Steam
Bigger, paid, theme-only (the in-game coins aren't real).

- ⭐ **[One Man's Trash](https://store.steampowered.com/app/3692070/One_Mans_Trash/)** —
  Satirical dig-'em-up: a washed-up crypto bro vacuums a landfill for a lost "PitCoin"
  hard drive — inspired by the real James Howells 8,000-BTC saga. `🎮 Steam` `💸 Paid` `👍 84% • Very Positive`
- ⭐ **[Crypto Miner Tycoon Simulator](https://store.steampowered.com/app/1859290/Crypto_Miner_Tycoon_Simulator/)** —
  Build rigs and run a mining business across scenarios; the best-reviewed of the
  mining tycoons. `🎮 Steam` `💸 Paid` `👍 83% • Very Positive`
- **[Blockchain Tycoon](https://store.steampowered.com/app/824450/Blockchain_Tycoon/)** —
  Mining/management economic sim: build a farm, mine, HODL or trade. `🎮 Steam` `💸 Paid`
- **[Bitcoin Tycoon — Mining Simulation Game](https://store.steampowered.com/app/734570/Bitcoin_Tycoon__Mining_Simulation_Game/)** —
  Start mining in "early 2014" and build a BTC empire. `🎮 Steam` `💸 Paid`
- **[Bitcoin Mining Tycoon](https://store.steampowered.com/app/884500/Bitcoin_Mining_Tycoon/)** —
  Colorful tap-to-mine farm builder. `🎮 Steam` `💸 Paid`
- **[Call of Bitcoin](https://store.steampowered.com/app/836260/Call_of_Bitcoin/)** —
  Tiny action platformer: collect bitcoins, blast monsters. `🎮 Steam` `💸 Paid`

## 📚 Learn Bitcoin (playable)

- ⭐ **[The Bitcoin Game](https://thebitcoingame.io/)** — Polished classroom Proof-of-Work
  sim: pick a nonce, hash, and race to a valid block across 10 rounds. `🌐 Browser` `🆓 Free` `📚 Learn`
- **[SHAmory](https://shamory.com/)** — Memory/card game (and picture books) that teach
  mining and Proof-of-Work. `🎲 Tabletop` `📚 Learn` _(see [Tabletop](#-tabletop--kids))_

## ⚡ Earn real sats (Lightning)
**A different category.** These pay out real Bitcoin (prize draws / rewards) rather than
being "play for fun." Listed for completeness — kept separate on purpose.

- **[THNDR Games](https://www.thndr.games/)** — The leading Lightning game studio; its
  titles (Club Bitcoin: Solitaire, Bitcoin Bounce, Tetro Tiles, Clinch) pay sats. `📱 Mobile` `⚡ Earns sats`
- **[ZBD](https://zbd.gg/)** — Lightning rewards platform & SDK behind ~40 "earn sats"
  games. `📱 Mobile` `⚡ Earns sats`
- **[Bitcoin Miner](https://zbd.gg/z/earn)** — Popular idle mining sim that pays real
  sats. `📱 Mobile` `⚡ Earns sats` _(Fumb Games, via ZBD)_
- **[SaruTobi](https://www.mandelduck.com/sarutobi/)** — The **first mobile Bitcoin game
  (2013)**; fling a monkey, collect sats. `📱 Mobile` `⚡ Earns sats`

## 🪦 Historical / no longer Bitcoin
Kept for the record — do **not** treat these as live Bitcoin games.

- **Lightnite** — the 2019/2020 "Lightning Fortnite" (Satoshi's Games) **migrated off
  Bitcoin to Solana NFTs**.
- **satoshis.games** — the original Lightning play-to-earn launcher (BCraft, Lightning
  Agar…) is **defunct**; the company pivoted to web3.
- **Bitcoin Bounty Hunt** — a first-person shooter that paid sats over Lightning;
  **discontinued (2021)**.
